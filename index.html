SUBSYSTEM 01 STACK: MEDIAPIPE + OPENCV + SCIKIT-LEARN

Attention Monitoring & Facial Landmark Telemetry

Algorithmic Mechanics

Continuous webcam processing estimates student attention states using Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR), Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR for yawning/fatigue), PERCLOS (Percentage of Eye Closure), and head pose/gaze angles. When attention falls below calibrated thresholds during technical explanation intervals, timestamps are catalogued for spaced re-engagement.

RESEARCH DOMAIN & RIGOR

Academic Foundation Grounded in Real Learning Needs.

An intersection of accessible technical education, affective computing, conversational AI, knowledge graphs, sign language linguistics, and assistive IoT hardware.

01

Literature Survey

Investigated state-of-the-art studies in multimodal affective computing, facial landmark estimation (EAR/MAR), conversational intelligent tutoring systems (ITS), knowledge graph path traversals, and deep learning-based sign language recognition (WLASL, Sign MNIST).

02

Research Gap

Contemporary e-learning platforms treat accessibility, student engagement telemetry, and personalized remedial tutoring as fragmented silos rather than connecting them into an integrated, curriculum-aligned feedback framework for deaf learners.

03

Research Problem

Deaf and hard-of-hearing students studying the Sri Lankan G.C.E. O/L ICT syllabus face profound barriers with audio-dependent lessons, dense textual terminology, lack of visual sign explanations, and unaddressed concept gaps when attention drifts.

04

Methodological Pipeline

Iterative research engineering encompassing data preprocessing, model training & cross-validation, API integration between microservices, latency benchmarking, and syllabus-aligned evaluation with ICT educators.

05

Technology Ecosystem

Modern stack combining FastAPI backend microservices, React & Vite frontends, MongoDB document stores, MediaPipe Face & Hand Mesh, OpenCV pipelines, TensorFlow/Keras deep learning models, and ESP32 hardware via BLE.

06

Academic Motivation

Bridging the digital divide in national technical education by building accessible, responsive, and empowering AI tools that respect deaf culture and foster self-directed technical mastery.

RESEARCH MOTIVATION

Empowering Understanding, Not Merely Broadcasting Content

Standard video tutorials rely heavily on spoken narration. Deaf and hard-of-hearing learners are forced to divide attention between reading hurried subtitles and studying complex software diagrams. When gaze leaves the screen or fatigue sets in, critical technical concepts are permanently missed.

SignLearn AI directly solves this by treating attention telemetry, knowledge graphs, adaptive dialog, and visual signing as one unified, supportive tutor.

CORE OBJECTIVES
  • Continuous Attention Telemetry: Track EAR, MAR, PERCLOS, and head pose to detect interval-level attention lapses in ICT video lessons.
  • Curriculum Knowledge Graph: Map 15 seed O/L ICT concepts and synthesize questions using the GQSA 60/25/15 ratio.
  • Emotion-Aware Conversational Tutoring: Deliver tailored explanations, revision notes, and mastery metrics with offline local fallback.
  • Multimodal Sign & Haptic Feedback: Provide text-to-gloss avatar demonstrations and silent ESP32 wristband vibration alerts.
